JURISDICTION RELATED DETAILS ABOUT THE DISTRICT OF
GHAZIABAD AND GAUTAM BUDDHA NAGAR
Noida and Greater Noida West, also known as Noida Extension, are two of the
most rapidly growing urban clusters of the National Capital Region (NCR).
With over 160 notified sectors in Noida and several high-density residential
sectors in Greater Noida West, lakhs of residents today call these areas home.
What many people are not always aware of is that all disputes, legal matters,
and proceedings arising within these areas fall under the jurisdiction of the
District Court Gautam Buddha Nagar, popularly referred to as the Surajpur
District Court. The city of Noida is planned with 163 designated sectors,
numbered from 1 to 168, with a few missing numbers such as 13, 103, 109, 111,
and 114. These sectors are divided into residential, institutional, industrial and
commercial zones. Over the years, certain sectors have emerged as densely
populated residential belts such as Sectors 12, 22, 27, 29, 34, 50, 51, 61, 62, 71,
72, 76, 77, 78, 79, 93, 93A, 137, 142, 143, and 150. These localities not only
house large apartment societies and independent homes but also witness
significant commercial and social activity. Importantly, any civil, criminal,
matrimonial, or property-related dispute arising in these Noida sectors is to be
addressed before the District Court at Surajpur. Greater Noida West has its own
distinct set of notified sectors such as Sector 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 16B, 16C,
18, and 19, along with major institutional and commercial zones like Tech Zone
IV and Knowledge Parks IV and V. This region is one of the fastest growing
residential hubs in Gautam Buddha Nagar, with high-rise group housing
societies and large township projects. Like Noida, all disputes arising in Greater
Noida West sectors also come under the exclusive jurisdiction of the Surajpur
District Court. Ghaziabad, a key urban district adjoining Delhi and Noida, falls
under the jurisdiction of the Ghaziabad District Court, with several prominent
residential and commercial hubs such as Indirapuram, Vaishali, and Vasundhara
forming some of the most densely populated and sought-after areas.
Indirapuram, known for its well-planned societies and gated communities,
includes popular residential clusters like Shipra Suncity, Amrapali Village, Gaur
Green City, Niti Khand, Ahinsa Khand, Nyay Khand, and Rajhans Society,
which are home to a large number of families, professionals, and working
individuals commuting to Delhi and Noida. Vaishali, another prime residential
locality, is recognized for its high-rise apartments and established
neighborhoods such as Vaishali Sector-1 to 6, Saya Zenith, Cloud 9, Mahagun
Apartments, and Supertech Residency, all of which contribute to its bustling
population and growing need for professional services. Vasundhara,
strategically connected through NH-24 and metro links, is equally significant,
comprising societies and sectors like Sector-1 to 19, Jaipuria Sunrise, Ashiana
Greens, and Gardenia Glamour, which make it a hub of both middle-class and
upper-middle-class households. These areas collectively form some of the
busiest and most vibrant residential pockets of Ghaziabad, where residents
frequently seek legal services ranging from family and matrimonial law to
property disputes, civil litigation, and criminal matters, all within the purview of
the Ghaziabad District Court. By reflecting the real essence of these
neighborhoods, their residential societies, connectivity, and community
life—the description naturally aligns with what people search for when looking
for advocates or law firms in Indirapuram, Vaishali, or Vasundhara, ensuring
better visibility while maintaining an informative and professional tone.
